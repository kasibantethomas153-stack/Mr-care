About Kikoma Coffee Traders

Kikoma Coffee Traders is a local coffee business in Kikoma, Sembabule District, in Uganda’s Central Region. We work directly with growers to make selling coffee simple and fair, and we supply cafés, shops and households with fresh coffee.
